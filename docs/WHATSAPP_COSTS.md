## 2. Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API 2026 Pricing Structure

When graduating from the free test sandbox to a live production phone number, Meta charges based on **message category**:

| Category | Typical Cost in India (per message) | 24-Hour Customer Service Window | Use Case in AASRA |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Service Messages** | **FREE (₹0.00)** | **FREE** | When farmer sends a query and AASRA replies (weather, spray window, crop diagnosis, mandi rates) |
| **Utility Messages** | **~₹0.12 – ₹0.35** ($0.004 – $0.008) | **FREE within 24h** | Proactive emergency weather alerts & spray notifications initiated by system outside 24h window |
| **Authentication Messages** | **~₹0.15 – ₹0.40** | Charged | OTP or password resets (not needed for WhatsApp link) |
| **Marketing Messages** | **~₹0.80 – ₹1.10** | Charged | Promotional announcements (AASRA does not send marketing) |

### Key Economic Advantage for AASRA:
Because farmers actively message AASRA with daily queries (*"Barish hogi?", "Spray window batao"*), **the 24-hour service window is opened by the farmer**. All conversational agronomic advice, image diagnoses, and voice queries delivered in response are **completely FREE**.

## 3. Production Scaling Projections (1,000 Active Farmers)

Assuming a pilot of 1,000 registered farmers:
* **Inbound Farmer Queries**: ~5,000 queries/month → **₹0.00** (All within Service Window).

* **Total Estimated Operational Cost for 1,000 Farmers**: **~₹300 – ₹500/month ($3.50 – $6.00/month)**.
