1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & PLATFORM VISION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AASRA is an enterprise-grade, 100% Google Cloud AI-powered agricultural intelligence
and yield protection platform built for Syngenta Biologicals. AASRA directly bridges
live hyper-local meteorological telemetry, physical agronomic stress models, and
high-fidelity Google Cloud generative AI (Gemini 2.0 Flash, Gemini Vision, Chirp 3 HD)
to deliver actionable, voice-enabled assistance to farmers while providing
quantitative proof of Return on Biological Investment (ROBI).

The platform transforms agricultural management from "reactive damage response"
to "hyper-local pre-emptive biostimulant execution," allowing smallholder farmers
and enterprise agronomists to protect yield potential against extreme climate
events (night heat stress, drought, frost, soil moisture deficits).


1.1 THE 6-PHASE OPERATIONAL EXECUTION LOOP (HOW IT WORKS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AASRA operates on a continuous 6-phase agronomic lifecycle:

  [LISTEN] ---> [UNDERSTAND] ---> [ADVISE (PS-04)] ---> [RECORD] ---> [MEASURE] ---> [PROVE (PS-07)]

Phase 1: LISTEN (Hyper-Local Telemetry Ingestion)
  - Ingests hourly 2m max/min/mean temperature, precipitation, soil moisture (0-10cm),
    and evapotranspiration via Meteoblue Dataset API (`NEMSGLOBAL` & `ERA5` domains).
  - Fetches GDD accumulation, hydric stress, and optimal spray/planting windows
    from Syngenta CE Hub API.

Phase 2: UNDERSTAND (Biophysical Stress Evaluation)
  - Evaluates 9 deterministic agronomic algorithms (`backend/app/services/agriculture/engine.py`):
    * Daytime Heat Stress Score (0-9 scale)
    * Nighttime Heat Stress Score (0-9 scale, threshold >25°C)
    * Frost Stress Score (0-9 scale)
    * Drought Risk Index (DI = (P - E) + SM/T)
    * Growing Degree Days (GDD = max(0, (Tmax+Tmin)/2 - Tbase))
    * Nitrogen Use Efficiency (NUE)
    * Yield Risk (YR)
  - Evaluates BRS Spray Safety Gate (Delta-T between 2°C and 8°C, wind <15km/h).

Phase 3: ADVISE (PS-04 Multilingual Google AI Advisory)
  - Receives farmer voice (Google STT) or leaf photo uploads.
  - Injects live weather, crop stage, and field telemetry into prompt context.
  - Generates advice via Google Gemini 2.0 Flash (`gemini-2.0-flash`) in 12 languages
    with explainable "Why this recommendation?" rationales and confidence scores (92-96%).
  - Synthesizes audio responses via Google Chirp 3 HD Speech API (`/api/chat/google-tts`).
  - Scans foliage for thermal scorch & disease via Google Gemini 2.0 Flash Vision.

Phase 4: RECORD (Biological Intervention Logging)
  - Logs Syngenta biostimulant applications (Stress Buster, Nutrient Booster, Yield Booster)
    with application date, dosage, and crop growth stage into persistent storage (`/journal`).

Phase 5: MEASURE (Weather-Adjusted Yield Attribution)
  - Calculates night heat degradation loss: ΔY_heat = Y_base * max(0, T_night - 25) * 0.028 * Δt.
  - Calculates Syngenta Stress Buster recovery gain: Y_bio_gain = ΔY_heat * 0.75.
  - Evaluates spray delay decay (12% protection loss per day delayed in What-If Simulator).

Phase 6: PROVE (PS-07 Financial ROBI Evidence Cards)
  - Computes Net Income Gain (INR/acre) = (Y_bio_gain * Market Price) - Spray Cost.
  - Computes ROBI Ratio (%) = (Net Income Gain / Spray Cost) * 100.
  - Renders exportable, verifiable ROBI Evidence Proof Cards on `/impact`.

4. MATHEMATICAL & AGRONOMIC FORMULATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AASRA implements deterministic biophysical equations to quantify climate stress
and biological protection.

4.1 Night Heat Stress Degradation (PS-07 Core)
When night temperatures exceed 25°C during crop flowering (e.g., Soybean R2 stage),
yield potential degrades at 2.8% per degree-hour:

    ΔY_heat = Y_base * max(0, T_night - 25) * 0.028 * Δt_hours

4.2 Biological Protection Gain (Syngenta Stress Buster)
Syngenta Stress Buster restores up to 75% of heat-induced yield loss:

    Y_bio_gain = ΔY_heat * ε_efficacy    (where ε_efficacy = 0.75)

4.3 Spray Application Delay Penalty (What-If Simulator)
Each day of delayed biostimulant spray application degrades efficiency by 12%:

    ε_delayed = ε_efficacy * (1 - 0.12 * delay_days)

4.4 Net Yield Formulation
    Y_final = Y_baseline - ΔY_heat + Y_bio_gain + ΔY_soil_water

4.5 Return on Biological Investment (ROBI) Formulas
    Net Income Gain (INR/acre) = (Y_bio_gain * P_market) - C_spray
    ROBI Ratio (%) = (Net Income Gain / C_spray) * 100

    *Example Soybean Benchmark (Bhopal):*
    - Market Price (P_market): INR 4,600 / quintal
    - Spray Cost (C_spray): INR 1,280 / acre
    - Yield Gain (Y_bio_gain): +0.60 q/acre
    - Gross Gain: 0.60 * 4,600 = INR 2,760 / acre
    - Net Income Gain: 2,760 - 1,280 = INR 1,480 / acre
    - ROBI Ratio: 215%

4.6 Drought Risk Index (DI)
    DI = (Precipitation - Evapotranspiration) + (SoilMoisture / Temperature)

4.7 Growing Degree Days (GDD)
    GDD = max(0, (T_max + T_min)/2 - T_base)

6. SYSTEM QUALITY ATTRIBUTES & OPERATIONAL STANDARDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AASRA adheres to strict enterprise software standards:

1. SECURITY & KEY ISOLATION
   - 0 Key Leakage: Secrets reside strictly in `backend/.env` (gitignored).
   - Backend Proxying: Frontend calls `/api/*` endpoints; raw API keys are never
     sent to client browsers.
   - Standard JWT authentication with configurable expiration.

2. RELIABILITY & GRACEFUL DEGRADATION
   - 100% Google AI Engine: Powered exclusively by Google Gemini 2.0 Flash REST & SDK.
   - API Adapter Resiliency: Automatic fallback to simulated agronomic data with
     `"is_demo": true` tag if Meteoblue or CE Hub APIs time out or return 404/400.

3. PERFORMANCE & CONCURRENCY
   - Async Execution: Fast non-blocking REST calls powered by `asyncio.to_thread`.
   - Production Build Speed: Next.js frontend builds cleanly in <9 seconds with 0
     TypeScript errors.

4. USER EXPERIENCE & ACCESSIBILITY
   - 12 Language Regionalization: Instant script and voice switching.
   - Provenance Badging: Every metric displays data origin (`LIVE Meteoblue`,
     `LIVE CE Hub`, `AI GENERATED (Gemini)`, `MODELLED AASRA`).
